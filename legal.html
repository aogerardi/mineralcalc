1 · Privacy Policy

Information we collect

MineralCalc is a static website with no user accounts and no login. We collect personal information in only one situation:

  • Waitlist email address. If you enter your email to join the Lease Analyzer waitlist, that email address is submitted to and stored by our form provider so we can notify you when the feature launches. Providing it is entirely optional.

Information we do not collect

  • Your calculator inputs. All royalty, acreage, production, lease, and check-verification calculations run locally in your browser. These values are never transmitted to, logged by, or stored on any server we control.
  • Lease documents you analyze. The Lease Analyzer reads your PDF using code running inside your own browser. The file is never uploaded, never sent to any server, and never retained after you leave or reload the page.
  • Well lookups. Searching a section downloads a public data file for that county to your browser and matches it locally. We do not receive or log the section you searched.
  • Shared links. When you use the "Share Link" feature, your inputs are encoded into the URL itself. That link is created on your device — we do not receive a copy. The data travels only to whomever you choose to send the link to.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, analytics profiling, or third-party marketing cookies.

Local storage

MineralCalc uses your browser's local storage for two things, both of which stay on your device and are never transmitted to us:

  • Your last calculator scenario, so your inputs are still there when you come back. Clear it at any time with the "Reset Defaults" button, or through your browser settings.
  • A waitlist flag, if you sign up, so the site does not show you the signup form again. It contains no personal information.

Third-party services

To operate the site, we rely on a few third parties, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Formspree — processes and stores waitlist email submissions.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website and may log standard request data such as IP addresses for security and operational purposes.
  • Google Fonts — serves the site's typefaces; Google may receive your IP address when fonts load.

How we use your email

A waitlist email address is used only to contact you about the Lease Analyzer feature, including launch notice and early-access details. We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address. You may ask us to delete it at any time by emailing alexogerardi@gmail.com.

Lease Analyzer

The Lease Analyzer reads a lease PDF entirely within your browser and screens it for common terms and clauses. Because the parsing happens on your device, there is no upload step and no server copy of your lease — we never see the document, its contents, or the results.

The analyzer matches known lease language patterns. It is a screening aid, not a substitute for legal review: it can miss non-standard drafting, riders, and exhibits, and clauses it does not flag may still be present. Have a licensed landman or oil and gas attorney review any lease before signing.

Oklahoma well data

The well lookup uses a periodically refreshed snapshot of public records published by the Oklahoma Corporation Commission (completion and intent-to-drill filings). The snapshot is served as static files from this site; the date it was built is shown in the lookup. Initial test rates are operator-reported and describe a well's early performance, not its average or current production. Verify current well status with the OCC directly.

Children

MineralCalc is intended for adults making decisions about mineral and royalty interests. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves — particularly when the Lease Analyzer launches. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Estimates only — not professional advice

MineralCalc produces estimates based on assumptions you enter. It does not constitute financial, investment, legal, tax, engineering, or accounting advice, and it does not create any professional or fiduciary relationship. Always consult a licensed petroleum engineer, landman, certified public accountant, or oil and gas attorney before making any lease, sale, purchase, or investment decision regarding mineral rights.

No guarantee of accuracy

Royalty income depends on many real-world variables this tool cannot fully capture — actual well performance and decline behavior, volatile commodity prices, specific lease language (including post-production deduction and shut-in clauses), title and ownership complexity, regulatory action, and operator decisions. Results may differ materially from actual outcomes. Severance tax rates, market benchmarks, well records, and other reference figures are provided for convenience and may not reflect current law, current production, or current conditions; verify them with the relevant authority.

The Lease Analyzer's findings are automated pattern matches and may be incomplete or wrong for your document. The Oklahoma well data is a third-party public snapshot that may be outdated or contain errors originating with the filings themselves. Do not rely on either as the sole basis for a decision.
