The short version: MineralCalc runs entirely in your web browser. Your calculator inputs are never sent to us or stored on a server. 1 · Privacy Policy

Information we collect

MineralCalc is a static website with no user accounts and no login. We collect personal information in only one situation:

  • Waitlist email address. If you enter your email to join the Lease Analyzer waitlist, that email address is submitted to and stored by our form provider so we can notify you when the feature launches. Providing it is entirely optional.

Information we do not collect

  • Your calculator inputs. All royalty, acreage, production, lease, and check-verification calculations run locally in your browser. These values are never transmitted to, logged by, or stored on any server we control.
  • Shared links. When you use the "Share Link" feature, your inputs are encoded into the URL itself. That link is created on your device — we do not receive a copy. The data travels only to whomever you choose to send the link to.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, analytics profiling, or third-party marketing cookies.

Local storage

If you join the waitlist, we store a single flag in your browser's local storage so the site remembers not to show you the signup form again. This flag contains no personal information and never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Third-party services

To operate the site, we rely on a few third parties, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Formspree — processes and stores waitlist email submissions.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website and may log standard request data such as IP addresses for security and operational purposes.
  • Google Fonts — serves the site's typefaces; Google may receive your IP address when fonts load.

How we use your email

A waitlist email address is used only to contact you about the Lease Analyzer feature, including launch notice and early-access details. We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address. You may ask us to delete it at any time by emailing alexogerardi@gmail.com.

Lease Analyzer (not yet available)

The Lease Analyzer is a planned future feature that would let you upload a lease document for analysis. It is not live today and does not currently collect or process any documents. When it launches, it will be covered by its own data-handling notice describing how uploaded files are processed and retained, presented to you before you use it.

Children

MineralCalc is intended for adults making decisions about mineral and royalty interests. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves — particularly when the Lease Analyzer launches. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Estimates only — not professional advice

MineralCalc produces estimates based on assumptions you enter. It does not constitute financial, investment, legal, tax, engineering, or accounting advice, and it does not create any professional or fiduciary relationship. Always consult a licensed petroleum engineer, landman, certified public accountant, or oil and gas attorney before making any lease, sale, purchase, or investment decision regarding mineral rights.

No guarantee of accuracy

Royalty income depends on many real-world variables this tool cannot fully capture — actual well performance and decline behavior, volatile commodity prices, specific lease language (including post-production deduction and shut-in clauses), title and ownership complexity, regulatory action, and operator decisions.
